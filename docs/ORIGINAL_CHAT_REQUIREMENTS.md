# Original Budget App history — product requirements only

Source: the owner-provided pasted chat history, reviewed November 6, 2026. This is a privacy-safe requirements extraction, not a copy of the private chat. Personal login/account/workspace details and unverified older assistant claims about Lovable ownership, provisioning and publication are deliberately excluded. The private raw upload stays outside the repository. Newer user decisions in this coding conversation take precedence.

## Dashboard / Forecast

- Show current account balances, net worth and upcoming bills/expenses.
- Show multiple savings accounts, goal status, debt standings and an optional debt snowball.
- Show credit/debt/budget hints grounded in the user's records; live coaching requires a real secured provider.
- Offer shortcuts to the other utilities.
- Visualize budget, savings and debt progress.
- Remind about birthdays, holidays and anniversaries two weeks ahead.

## Budget Calendar

- Display bill due dates, expected clearing dates and actual cleared dates separately.
- Display expected paydays and received income.
- Allow custom important dates and local/national holidays.

## Cash Flow / Ledger

- Provide a checkbook across manual accounts, credit cards and loan payments.
- Display line-by-line running balances.
- Optionally sync supported checking/savings, credit, loans and retirement accounts.
- Keep manual entry available without bank permissions.
- Support Manual, Connected and Hybrid workflows per account without restricting manual functionality.
- Maintain one authoritative balance instead of independently editing account and debt totals.
- Keep due, scheduled, paid and actually cleared dates distinct.

## Debt and Savings

- Show the entire debt footprint and choices for prioritizing repayment.
- Offer credit education without guaranteed credit-score claims.
- Support savings goals such as emergency funds, travel, gifts and retirement.
- Keep savings earmarks distinct from additional assets.

## Pip and onboarding

- Provide a conversational place for explanations and proposed service actions.
- Gather useful setup information while allowing sections to be skipped.
- Keep an Inbox with actionable reminders for incomplete setup.
- Allow custom account/category/expense names, dates and terminology.
- Use a modern, approachable interface with useful shortcuts and an original robot companion.
- Treat Toshl’s approachable mascot concept as inspiration; do not copy its branding or claim a current competitor review was performed.
- Keep bank integration, live AI and paid services truthful about implementation and configuration.
- Keep previews and test data private; browser-local prototypes are not secure customer deployments.

## Private raw workbook

The owner supplied an unfinished modified workbook as a conceptual reference, not an authoritative specification or a completed calculation engine. It was inspected locally for structure/formula families without importing personal records into LedgerBot. No macros, external workbook links or literal broken-reference formulas were observed in that structural check. This is not proof that workbook formulas, cached results or financial assumptions are correct. No original transactions, names, balances, rates or private account identifiers are copied into fixtures or reports. Test values are invented.

The master specification already represents these broad requirements. The line inventory tracks this extraction separately so Calendar, debt/savings planning and live integrations cannot disappear behind successful manual-ledger tests.
